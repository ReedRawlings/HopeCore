**HopeCore App Development**: HopeCore is an LLM first development application. When designing the models and views do so for LLMs and Agents rather than humans. Humans will review updates and run the app in simulators but do not need to deep understanding of the back end architecture.
**File Comments**: When creating new models and views or making changes, leave comments for future agents to understand your design choices and implementation strategies
**Adherence**: Follow what is outlined in the technical_spec.md and designdoc.md do not add additional components to either of those documents unless a human specifically says so. 
**Clarity**: If ever confused pause what you're doing and ask your human for clarification
